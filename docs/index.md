# :material-vote: Re-Elect Christine Seibert as your New Hampshire State Representative

![Image title](assets/images/banner.jpg){ loading=lazy }

## :material-image-area: Hillsborough District 21

As your State Representative, I will continue to listen and fight for your concerns, promote values
that benefit our community, and bring common sense to the State House.

## :material-account-supervisor-circle: Serving you honestly and thoughtfully with results in mind

I, **[Christine Seibert](https://gencourt.state.nh.us/house/members/member.aspx?pid=9077)**, am a
proud member of the [Democratic Party](https://democrats.org/) who is actively serving in the
[New Hampshire House of Representatives](https://gencourt.state.nh.us/house/), representing
*Hillsborough 21 (Manchester Ward 1)*, and I am asking for your vote for your support in this upcoming
election.

- State Primary Election - September 8, 2026
- State General Election - November 3, 2026

As the Ranking Member for the Democrats in the
[State-Federal Relations and Veterans Affairs Committee](https://www.gencourt.state.nh.us/house/committees/committeedetails.aspx?id=16),
we have worked hard to find common ground when it comes to supporting our Veterans, National
Guard, and their families. This is personal for me, coming from a family of Veterans where both my
Father (US Army Veteran) and Husband (US Navy Veteran) proudly served our country. Collectively,
we **PASSED** [HB1287](https://gc.nh.gov/bill_status/billinfo.aspx?id=2010&inflect=2)
revised the definition of "Veteran" in the State RSA to include both Honorable and General
(Under Honorable Conditions) discharge. This allows for all honorably discharged veterans the
rights to receive state benefits such as veteran plates and more.

As a member of our County Delegation, I was honored to be selected to on the Hillsborough County
Executive Committee, as well Chairing one of the Budget Subcommittees.

As the Secretary of the NH House Progressive Caucus, I tracked our members' votes for
leadership to identify new members and ensure the integrity of progressives in the State House.

Having sponsored or co-sponsored around thirty-two (32) bills/resolutions in this past term (2025-2026), six (6)
of which were Signed, Enacted, or Adopted into law, and two (2) House Resolutions Passed and were
Adopted with an Admendment. I am very proud of the work I did for you in this past term in office.
